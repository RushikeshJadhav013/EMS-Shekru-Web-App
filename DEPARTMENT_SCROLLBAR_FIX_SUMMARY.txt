DEPARTMENT DIALOG SCROLLBAR FIX - QUICK SUMMARY
================================================================================

PROBLEM:
- Edit/Update department dialog had scrollbar touching top & bottom edges
- Poor visual appearance with no spacing around scrollbar
- Scrollbar extended to full height of dialog window

ROOT CAUSE:
- overflow-y-auto was applied directly to DialogContent
- Scrollbar appeared at the edge of the container
- No separation between scrollbar and dialog boundaries

SOLUTION:
✅ Restructured dialog with separate header and content sections
✅ Header section is fixed (doesn't scroll)
✅ Content section has scrollbar with proper padding
✅ Scrollbar now has spacing from top and bottom edges

STRUCTURE CHANGES:

Before:
<DialogContent className="max-h-[80vh] overflow-y-auto">
  <DialogHeader>...</DialogHeader>
  <Form>...</Form>
</DialogContent>

After:
<DialogContent className="max-h-[80vh] p-0 flex flex-col">
  <div className="px-6 pt-6 pb-2">
    <DialogHeader>...</DialogHeader>
  </div>
  <div className="overflow-y-auto px-6 pb-6 flex-1">
    <Form>...</Form>
  </div>
</DialogContent>

BENEFITS:
✅ Scrollbar has proper spacing from edges
✅ Header remains visible while scrolling
✅ Professional, clean appearance
✅ Better user experience
✅ Consistent padding throughout

FILES CHANGED:
- Frontend/src/pages/departments/DepartmentManagement.tsx
  * Create Department Dialog
  * Edit Department Dialog

DEPLOYMENT:
